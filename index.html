About The NGO

InAmigos Foundation is a youth-driven NGO focused on spreading awareness, helping communities, and encouraging social responsibility among students and volunteers. The organization works on multiple social initiatives including educational awareness, healthcare support, community welfare, and social campaigns aimed at creating positive change.
